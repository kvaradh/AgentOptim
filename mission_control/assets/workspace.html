How the system thinks

Structured disagreement, not a black box.

The activity adapter is selected per mission target, while ADME and safety roles remain reusable. This build ships with DRD2; another receptor requires its own validated activity scorer. Confidence is a reproducible within-slate margin, not a probability. Cora can block a candidate at the fixed hERG gate, and Perry records why every option advanced or failed.

The Agent Perry loop

From molecule to the next useful experiment.

Every round leaves a replayable decision receipt.

  1. 01Input

    Resolve a known drug to a validated molecular structure.

  2. 02Design

    Generate nearby molecular edits rather than arbitrary structures.

  3. 03Predict

    Evaluate several ADMET objectives on a common display scale.

  4. 04Debate

    Specialists independently rank options, then challenge one another’s evidence.

  5. 05Gate

    Cardiac-safety and reliability rules can veto an option regardless of its average score.

  6. 06Decide

    Perry documents the trade-off and creates the next-round brief.

  7. 07Learn

    Value-of-information reasoning proposes the experiment most likely to change the decision.
